Pizza

Home

Description

With this recipe you can make a homemade pizza with a crispy crust. The steps below will guide you through it.

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour
  • 7 g dried yeast
  • 200 ml lukewarm water
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 150 ml tomato sauce
  • 150 g grated mozzarella
  • Toppings of your choice (for example salami, mushrooms or bell pepper)
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, yeast and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add the water and olive oil and knead for about 10 minutes until the dough is smooth.
  3. Cover the bowl and let the dough rise for 1 hour in a warm place.
  4. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  5. Roll out the dough on a baking tray lined with baking paper.
  6. Spread the tomato sauce over the dough and sprinkle with mozzarella.
  7. Add your toppings and the oregano.
  8. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
