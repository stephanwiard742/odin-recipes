Description

With this recipe you can bake rich and fudgy chocolate brownies

The steps below will guide you through it.

Ingredients

  • 200 g dark chocolate
  • 150 g butter
  • 200 g sugard
  • 3 eggs
  • 100 g flour
  • 30 g cocoa powder
  • 1 pinch of salt

Steps

  1. PPreheat the oven to 180°C and line a baking tin with baking paper.
  2. Melt the chocolate and butter together over low heat. Let it cool slightly.
  3. Whisk the eggs and sugar until light and fluffy.
  4. Stir the chocolate mixture into the eggs.
  5. Sift in the flour, cocoa powder and salt, and fold until just combined.
  6. Pour the batter into the baking tin and spread it evenly.
  7. Bake for 20–25 minutes. The middle should still be slightly soft.
  8. Let the brownies cool completely before cutting them into squares.
