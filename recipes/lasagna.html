Lasagna

Home

Description

With this recipe you can make a wonderful tasting lasagna in just a few steps. The steps below will guide you through it.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna sheets
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 400 g canned chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 50 g butter
  • 50 g flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • 150 g grated cheese (mozzarella or Gouda)
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Fry the onion and garlic in a pan with a little oil until soft.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  4. Stir in the chopped tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano and basil. Season with salt and pepper and let it simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. For the white sauce, melt the butter in a saucepan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  6. Slowly add the milk while stirring until the sauce is smooth and thick.
  7. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish and cover it with lasagna sheets.
  8. Add a layer of meat sauce, then a layer of white sauce, then lasagna sheets. Repeat until everything is used.
  9. Finish with white sauce and sprinkle the grated cheese on top.
  10. Bake the lasagna for 35–40 minutes until golden brown. Let it rest for 10 minutes before serving.
